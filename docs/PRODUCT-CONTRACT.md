# Little Red's Big Studio — Product Contract

## Product promise

Buddy operates the technical machinery. Users describe what they want and receive a verified, usable result without needing to know models, providers, endpoints, settings, dependencies, or recovery procedures.

## Platform

- Android-first and mobile-browser-first.
- No computer, Colab, notebook, terminal, or manual dependency setup required for ordinary use.
- Heavy workloads use autonomous remote execution and provider failover.
- Free/open/public resources are preferred; paid/API-key dependencies are not mandatory for the core experience.

## Identity and memory

- User identity is account-specific; never hard-code the creator's name for everyone.
- Support normal sign-in/sign-up and platform-supported biometric authentication.
- Buddy memory is user-scoped and designed for lifelong continuity: conversations, preferences, projects, decisions, lessons, Creative DNA, successes, failures, and relationships between assets.
- Memory must be honest: never claim recall when the underlying data is unavailable.
- Provide export/import/portability for user-owned creative memory and project metadata.

## Creative Project Graph

Projects relate songs, lyrics, vocals, instrumentals, images, characters, videos, thumbnails, Shorts, release campaigns, versions, and source/master assets. Results remain traceable to their source and version. Originals and highest-quality masters are never mutated by watermarking or derivatives.

## Buddy intelligence

Buddy can understand goals rather than only literal instructions; research the live web when useful; use multimodal context; critique work; identify blind spots; disagree constructively; choose the simplest high-quality method; plan multi-step projects; and remember why previous approaches succeeded or failed.

## Generation

Every major generation control must produce a real, validated artifact. This includes songs, lyrics, images, videos, voice cloning, voice conversion, vocal swapping, vocal separation, covers, avatars, lip sync, music videos, audio processing, and release assets.

For voice replacement, support both: (A) user voice sample + existing song -> replace original vocals while preserving the instrumental; and (B) user voice sample + instrumental + lyrics -> create the vocal performance. Buddy chooses technical settings automatically.

## Autonomous quality and recovery

The system plans, executes, validates, critiques, repairs, retries, fails over, and only reports success after an actual usable artifact passes appropriate checks. Resume from the last successful stage, prevent duplicate jobs, autosave, recover interrupted sessions/uploads, maintain versions, and retire unreliable providers/routes automatically.

## Provider selection

Use a capability/quality matrix considering task fit, quality, reliability, speed, availability, privacy, and resource requirements. Never expose provider switching to ordinary users. Models and public services are replaceable machinery.

## Free and paid access

- Every new account receives one seven-day all-access trial starting at verified account activation.
- During the trial, the user experiences the full product without an arbitrary credit counter and without export watermarking.
- Trial expiration is authoritative server-side/account-side and survives browser resets.
- Free tier remains genuinely useful after trial expiry and applies the Studio logo watermark to applicable exported generated content.
- Paid Buddy Unlimited is $10/month through the creator's configured Buy Me a Coffee membership entitlement.
- Paid entitlement must be verified server-side; browser storage alone cannot unlock paid features.
- Unlimited means no artificial credit counter, subject to legitimate service availability, abuse prevention, and infrastructure fair-use safeguards.

## Trial-abuse protection

Use layered, privacy-conscious signals: account verification, short-lived security records, device/session indicators, IP/network reputation, rapid-account-creation detection, generation-abuse detection, and rate limits. IP is a risk signal, never a sole identity key. Avoid invasive permanent fingerprinting. Shared households and mobile networks must not be casually blocked. Escalate verification only for high-risk activity.

## Watermarking

Use the canonical Little Red's Big Studio logo for free-tier exported generated content where technically meaningful. Watermarks must be applied to derivatives, never original/master artifacts. Video and audio require format-specific export pipelines; a player overlay is not considered a permanent exported watermark. Paid removal requires verified entitlement.

## Support

Creator support destinations: Cash App $LittleRedBigSmile; Buy Me a Coffee littleredbigsmile; YouTube Little Red Big Smile. Tips are optional and never required for access. Automated supporter shout-outs are opt-in only; never expose payment details. If automated recognition cannot be verified reliably, do not promise public shout-outs.

## Visual/product experience

Premium creative-instrument feel; animated canonical Buddy; animated logo; tasteful dripping-red visual identity; animated wallpaper; day/night modes; subtle edge lighting; distinct but unified creative realms; smooth transitions; reduced-motion support; large Android touch targets; clear generation states; real media previews; minimal clutter; no technical cockpit by default.

## Creative modes

Support a single universal creation flow plus specialist realms for sound, visuals, motion, research, and the project vault. Add Creative Director mode, Make It Great review/improvement, creative A/B testing, complete release/campaign generation, and proactive blind-spot detection without becoming intrusive.

## Privacy and trust

User files, memories, and Creative DNA are isolated by account. Do not use user material for training without explicit appropriate consent. Technical diagnostics belong in a hidden/admin layer. The user-facing experience reports useful outcomes, not raw provider errors or implementation details.

## Acceptance standard

The product is not considered finished merely because it installs, builds, or deploys. Critical paths must be exercised at runtime and a control is considered working only when it produces the promised usable outcome on the target Android/browser environment or transparently falls back to the best available alternative.
