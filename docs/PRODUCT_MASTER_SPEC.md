# Little Red's Big Studio — Product Master Specification

## Product promise
Buddy operates the technical machinery. Users ask for outcomes, not model configuration. Technical failures, provider selection, preprocessing, retries, quality checks, and failover remain invisible except in private diagnostics.

## Platform
- Android-first, browser-first, no computer required.
- No Colab requirement and no notebook workflow in the user product.
- No mandatory installation, API keys, or paid developer setup for ordinary use.
- Browser/device processing for lightweight work; free/public/open remote inference for heavier work; provider-agnostic orchestration.
- Free/open/public options are preferred, while task fit, quality, reliability, speed, privacy, and availability determine the best route.

## Buddy
- Natural continuous live voice conversation with a single-press conversation control.
- Natural pauses, timing, and breathing where supported by the selected voice route.
- User-specific name/profile; never hard-code Red as everyone's name.
- Lifelong memory architecture with account-scoped Creative DNA, preferences, projects, decisions, lessons, failures, and relationships.
- Honest recall: never claim to remember information that is unavailable.
- Live web search and research when current information is useful.
- Multimodal understanding across text, audio, image, video, documents, and project assets.
- Creative Director mode, intelligent disagreement, blind-spot detection, and "make it great" workflows.
- Buddy chooses tools/models automatically and can learn better workflows from verified outcomes.

## Creative Project Graph
Projects connect songs, lyrics, vocals, instrumentals, masters, artwork, characters, videos, Shorts, thumbnails, research, and release assets. Preserve source, master, derivatives, provenance, versions, and relationships where possible.

## Generation
Every generation feature must produce a real usable artifact, not merely a successful-looking control. Required capabilities include lyrics, complete songs, voice cloning, singing voice conversion, vocal replacement, vocal separation, voice selection/languages, image generation/editing/outpainting, consistent characters, avatars, video, music video, lip sync, audio editing, mixing/mastering, covers, thumbnails, Shorts, and release packages.

### Vocal workflows
1. Existing song + user's voice sample: separate original vocals, convert/replace vocals, synchronize, mix, QC, export.
2. Instrumental + user's voice sample + lyrics: generate/convert singing, synchronize, mix/master, QC, export. Lyrics may be generated or supplied manually.

## Reliability / self-healing
- Capability-aware provider/model routing.
- Health checks, retries, cooldowns, fresh-connection recovery, and automatic failover.
- Validate actual output before declaring success.
- Resume from the last successful stage.
- Autosave and recovery after refresh/backgrounding/interrupted uploads.
- Duplicate-job protection, cancellation, cleanup, queue/background jobs.
- Continuous benchmarking and retirement of unreliable routes.
- Hidden diagnostics and incident history.
- Never expose raw technical errors to ordinary users.

## Quality
Check generated media for corruption, emptiness, silence, clipping, synchronization, intelligibility, completeness, valid resolution/format, and other task-specific quality failures. Regenerate or switch route when QC fails.

## Account and privacy
- Normal sign-up/sign-in.
- Biometric authentication where supported by the platform/authentication layer.
- Account-isolated memory and Creative DNA.
- Export/import for portable Creative DNA where implemented.
- Privacy-conscious storage and retention; do not expose private payment or identity data.

## Trial and tiers
### Seven-day all-access trial
- Starts at the server-recorded moment the user officially completes signup/login and receives the welcome experience.
- Seven days of full Studio access with no feature/credit limits intended to let users genuinely try everything.
- Countdown displayed on the homepage and remains accurate across sessions/devices.
- No watermark during the trial.
- Projects and creations remain after trial expiry.

### Free tier after trial
- Useful free/open creative access remains available.
- Generated free-tier exports receive the Studio logo watermark.
- Original/master assets are not permanently contaminated by the watermark.

### Buddy Unlimited — $10/month
- Membership entitlement through the creator's Buy Me a Coffee membership.
- Watermark-free exports.
- Full Studio access subject to legitimate infrastructure availability and abuse safeguards, not an artificial credit counter.
- Entitlement must be verified server-side; never trust a browser-only paid flag.

## Trial abuse protection
Use layered, privacy-conscious signals rather than IP alone: account identity, verified email/phone where appropriate, device/session indicators, short-lived protected network/IP risk signals, rapid account creation patterns, generation abuse patterns, and provider resource safeguards. Do not permanently equate one IP with one person. Normal households/shared networks must remain usable. Do not use invasive fingerprinting as the sole identity mechanism.

## Watermark
Use the canonical Little Red's Big Studio logo for free-tier exported content. Make it tasteful and brand-forward. Paid entitlement removes the watermark. Original masters remain unwatermarked. Format-specific export pipelines must perform real watermarking; a player overlay is not sufficient for downloaded video.

## Creative intelligence
- "Make it great" evaluates the current project and improves weaknesses.
- "What am I missing?" identifies blind spots before important releases.
- Creative A/B testing can generate and compare alternatives.
- AI chooses when not to use generative AI and selects simpler high-quality tools when appropriate.
- AI remembers failed approaches and avoids repeating them.
- AI can simulate workflow scenarios and identify bottlenecks without pretending to predict the future.

## Release workflow
A single project can become a complete release package: master, artwork, lyrics, instrumental/acapella where applicable, full video, lyric video, visualizer, Shorts, thumbnail, title, description, tags/hashtags, community/social copy, and research-backed positioning.

## Non-negotiable acceptance standard
A feature is not complete because a button works, a request was submitted, or a build passed. It is complete only when the intended user outcome has been verified end-to-end on the actual supported Android/browser experience and failure paths recover or communicate honestly.
