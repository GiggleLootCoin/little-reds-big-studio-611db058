# Trial, Membership, Watermark and Support Policy

## Seven-day all-access trial
A verified new account receives one seven-day all-access trial. The authoritative start time is recorded for the account when signup/login activation completes. The homepage countdown is derived from that timestamp and remains accurate across refreshes, devices, and browser restarts.

The trial is designed to let users genuinely try the entire Studio. There is no artificial credit counter during the trial and generated exports are not watermarked.

## Abuse protection
The system must prevent simple multi-account farming without treating shared households, schools, workplaces, carrier NAT, or VPN users as automatically abusive. Account identity is primary. IP/network reputation, device/session signals, velocity, repeated account creation, and generation consumption are secondary risk signals. Security data should be minimized and retained only as necessary for abuse prevention.

High-risk activity may trigger additional verification or temporary resource protection. Ordinary users should not be exposed to technical risk scores.

## Free tier
After the trial, users retain their account, projects, memories, Creative DNA, and previously created assets. Free generation remains useful. Applicable generated exports receive the canonical Studio logo watermark.

## Buddy Unlimited
The paid membership target is $10/month through the configured Buy Me a Coffee membership. Paid status must be verified by a trusted backend entitlement flow. No browser-only flag may unlock paid features. Unlimited means no artificial monthly credit counter while still permitting infrastructure-level abuse controls and provider availability handling.
